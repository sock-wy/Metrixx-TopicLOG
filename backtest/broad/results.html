Study broad_v2 · 2026-08-26 to 2026-10-21 · 41 trading days · 212 topics

Which version of the Topic Log score picks the topics that matter the next day?

Each trading day at 15:00 ET the dashboard's pull was rebuilt from the backfill, every scoring variant ranked the topics that pass the gates, and the ranking was compared with what happened after the pick: the next session's price move, and the next day's Adanos mentions against the topic's normal level.

Result

  • w0 and v2 as shipped do no better than chance. On the holdout their rank IC is about zero for price moves and for attention.
  • The 1-day changes are the weak part, the data is not. How far a topic's mentions sit above its own 20-day normal predicts the next day's attention strongly (IC +0.35), and 3-day mention changes beat 1-day changes. Sentiment change has no signal, or a negative one.
  • Prediction-market inputs add little. Volume and open-interest changes are slightly positive and not significant; probability moves are about zero.
  • w1 is the new default. On the holdout it beats v2 by +0.09 (95% CI +0.03 to +0.14) IC on price moves and +0.26 (95% CI +0.20 to +0.33) on attention.
  • Momentum is still as good on price moves. w1 ties it there (-0.02 (95% CI -0.06 to +0.02)) and beats it on attention (+0.20 (95% CI +0.12 to +0.27)). Momentum is now in the score with weight 0.25.

What changed from w0 to w1

Attention
1-day buzz change → mentions on t−1 against the topic's own mean over t−20…t−2 (log ratio, Reddit + X + News). Weight 0.25 → 0.30.
Activity
1-day mention change → last 3 days against the 3 before. Weight 0.10.
Momentum
Reference column → in the score, weight 0 → 0.25.
Sentiment
0.15 → 0 (no signal, negative on attention).
Money flow / stock / move
0.20 / 0.10 / 0.20 → 0.15 / 0.10 / 0.10.
Missing inputs
Left out with weights rescaled → counted as the pool median (0.5). Same backtest result, and a topic with one strong input and nothing else can no longer top the list.
Quality multiplier
On → off by default (switch kept). With it on, w1 loses about a third of its edge.
Tiers
Rank by tier → labels only (switch kept). Ranking by tier did not help in any variant.
Gates
Unchanged: G2 needs prediction-market volume, G3 spread and zero-trade limits.

Limits

  • 41 trading days and about 80 gated topics a day. Effects smaller than about 0.05 IC are hard to tell from zero.
  • w1's inputs were chosen after looking at the input ICs over the full window, so its discovery numbers are not a clean test. The holdout agrees with discovery for every w1 variant.
  • Kalshi's backfill keeps long-dated events for their last 30 days only, so Kalshi inputs are thinner before late September. Polymarket has daily prices but no volume history, so Polymarket only enters through probability moves here.
  • Earnings dates are not in the backfill, so tier T1 for companies could not be tested. Cooldown (G4) is off in both the live dashboard and the backtest.
  • Next-day attention is persistent: a topic that is busy today is usually busy tomorrow. That is what makes the attention level useful for choosing a topic, and it is also why it says little about price moves.
