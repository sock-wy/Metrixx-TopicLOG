Topic stocks: what each dimension does

102 stocks and ETFs · 39 pick days (discovery 20, holdout 19) · 3,978 stock-days per version · pick at 15:00 ET · score = mean of dimensions E, A, D, M · main label: attention over the next 3 days · dimensions 5–8 not used

The four-dimension score picks topics well, but attention does nearly all the work. On the holdout, V1 put its pick at the 81th percentile of next-3-day attention (IC +0.36) and beat both momentum and yesterday's most-discussed stock. Attention alone scored higher (IC +0.45): removing A costs 0.16 of IC, while removing E, D or M changes it by less than 0.03. E decided the pick on 95% of holdout days without improving the ranking; on next-day price range it is the dimension that helps most (+0.044 over all days). Kalshi fields lowered IC on their 38 stocks.

Limits of this run

  • Dimensions are not centred. Mean value: E +0.26, A -0.01, D +0.22, M -0.16, because E2 and D2 are absolute values. The total averages only the dimensions a stock has, so a stock without M is not pulled down by M's negative mean: 95% of holdout picks had no M, against 73% of all stocks.
  • M coverage fell from 51% in discovery to 27% in the holdout (fewer Polymarket trades).
  • Kalshi fields were standardised against one pooled history, as pre-registered. That pool mixes the SPY/QQQ index ladders with company markets, and D4 points the wrong way (IC -0.13 on range).
  • The main label rewards attention that persists, which favours A by construction. On next-day range the total beats attention alone (+0.087 vs +0.037).
