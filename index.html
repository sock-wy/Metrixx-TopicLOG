Topic Log Playbook01 · The Topic Log
Chapter 01

The Topic Log

A three-sheet record that decides which ticker each daily MYCAST episode covers, and keeps enough history to check whether the choice was any good.

rawdecisionfeedback
Sheet 1

market_snapshots

1 row · contract · pull

Quotes, volume, open interest, contract structure and lifecycle. Answers "is this a real, usable price".

Sheet 2

topic_log

1 row · ticker · day

Adanos, prediction-market and eToro signals rolled up per ticker, then demand_score and rank.

Sheet 3

episode_log

1 row · episode

The call at the time, whether the editor agreed, whether it was producible, and how the audience responded.

sheet 3 tunes the weights used in sheet 2
Reading rule

An implied probability is never read alone. It is read with its spread (the error bar), its volume (whether anyone traded), and its contract type (what the number means).

Topic Log Playbook02 · The Daily Run
Chapter 02

The Daily Run

Six steps in a fixed order. Times are proposed, US Eastern, and assume the VPS snapshot job ran overnight.

  1. 06:30

    1 · Pull

    Latest Adanos and Kalshi snapshots land. Settled contracts are dropped.

    → 1_market_snapshots
  2. 07:00

    2 · Gate

    Candidates failing any hard gate are removed. The failed gate is recorded.

    → 2_topic_log · data_quality_flag
  3. 07:00

    3 · Score

    Compute demand_score and assign each surviving ticker a tier.

    → 2_topic_log · demand_score, rank
  4. 07:15

    4 · Pick

    The editor takes the top of the highest non-empty tier, or overrides with a reason code.

    → 3_episode_log · selection_mode
  5. 07:20

    5 · Angle

    Read the angle off the contract structure and confirm the material exists.

    → 3_episode_log · angle, material_sufficiency
  6. +7 days

    6 · Close

    Fill audience metrics, editor rating and how the market resolved.

    → 3_episode_log · views_7d … market_resolved_as

Topic Log Playbook03 · Hard Gates
Chapter 03

Hard Gates

Fail any one and the ticker is out for the day. Gates run before scoring, so a broken row never receives a rank.

G1

Freshness

Stale rows rank yesterday's news.

now − as_of ≤ 24h
G2

Market evidence

At least one open prediction-market contract with non-zero volume. Buzz alone is talk, not a market view.

count(open ∧ volume_24h > 0) ≥ 1
G3

Liquidity floor

Mostly zero-trade markets or very wide spreads make the implied probability meaningless.

zero_trade_market_pct ≤ 0.80 ∧ avg_spread ≤ 0.30
G4

Cooldown

Covered inside the window: skipped. Up to four days past it: scored at 0.85×.

days_since_covered > N, N = 3
G5

Not settled

A quote at 0.0005 or 0.9995 is an answer, not a forecast. Removed upstream in step 1.

market_status = open ∧ 0.01 ≤ implied_prob ≤ 0.99

Topic Log Playbook04 · demand_score
Chapter 04

demand_score

One number per ticker, built from what changed today. A name that is always hot is not today's news; a name that just got hot is.

demand_score = quality × cooldown × Σ wᵢ · pctrank(xᵢ) / Σ wᵢ
quality = spread_f × zero_trade_f × source_f
  spread_f     = clamp(1 − max(0, avg_spread − 0.05) / 0.5, 0.6, 1)
  zero_trade_f = 1 − 0.4 · zero_trade_market_pct
  source_f     = 1.0 if sources_available ≥ 2 else 0.9
ComponentRaw inputw0Captures
attention_shiftbuzz_delta_1d0.25A name that just got hot
activity_shifttrade_count Δ% 1d0.10More people trading, not just talking
money_flowmkt_volume_24h Δ% 1d0.20New money arriving today
money_stockmkt_open_interest Δ% 1d0.10Positions being held, not just traded
market_movemax(|prob_Δ1d|, range_1d/2)0.20The market changed its mind
sentiment_shift|sentiment_Δ1d|0.15Opinion moved, either way
  • Percentiles are taken within the day's surviving pool, because buzz points, contracts and probabilities share no unit.
  • A missing component is dropped and the remaining weights renormalised; it never counts as zero.
  • Direction does not enter the score. It belongs to the angle (Chapter 07).

Topic Log Playbook05 · Priority Tiers
Chapter 05

Priority Tiers

Pick from the highest tier that has anyone in it, then order by demand_score. Tiers stop a score built on attention alone from beating a name where money and an event line up.

T1

Event with money behind it

A scheduled catalyst, and the market is repricing.

catalyst ≤ 2 trading days ∧ (market_move ≥ p70 ∨ money_flow ≥ p70)
T2

Money moving

Betting is picking up and attention is following.

money_flow ≥ p70 ∧ attention_shift ≥ p50
T3

Attention only

More talking than betting. Good for a direction story, weak for a market story.

attention_shift ≥ p80
T4

Baseline

Passes the gates, no trigger fired. Ranked by score alone.

everything else
Tie-breakers, in order
  1. More sources_available
  2. Higher unique_traders, which guards against a few accounts manufacturing heat
  3. Deeper mkt_ladder_depth, since more angles are available
  4. Asset class not covered in the last two episodes

Fallback

If the needed material is missing, drop to the simpler angle and write it in production_note. Two fallbacks in a row for the same ticker lowers its material_sufficiency.

Topic Log Playbook08 · Overrides
Chapter 08

Overrides

The editor can overrule the board. Each override is logged with selection_mode = override, a reason code and one line of text. It is the fastest feedback the system gets.

CodeUse whenWhat it says about the score
BREAKING_NEWSMaterial news broke after the 07:00 buildSnapshot timing, not weights
SIMILAR_RECENTA related ticker or theme was covered recentlyCooldown should work at theme level
MATERIAL_GAPThe top pick cannot be produced well todaymaterial_sufficiency should enter the score
DATA_SUSPECTA number looks wrong on inspectionA gate is missing or too loose
EDITORIAL_MIXToo many recent episodes on one asset classTie-breaker 4 should become a gate
Rule of three

The same code three times within 14 days triggers a weight or gate review at the next weekly check.

Topic Log Playbook09 · Review Loop
Chapter 09

Review Loop

At about one episode a day, the sample is too small for regression for months. Until then, review by hand once a week.

Look at
  • Override rate and the codes behind it
  • avg_view_duration_pct and editor_rating by tier
  • Days rank 1 was skipped: did the replacement do better?
  • Calibration: implied prob at selection vs market_resolved_as
Change control
  • Any weight, threshold or tier change bumps weights_version
  • Never recompute demand_score_at_selection under new weights
  • Change one thing per week so its effect can be read
  • Record the change and reason in the repo changelog
Why avg_view_duration_pct

Of the audience metrics it is closest to "was this the right topic", because it depends least on title and thumbnail. views_7d mostly measures packaging.
